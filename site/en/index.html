01 / Why

28·04·202512:33 · Spain and Portugal

What if the internet goes down tomorrow?

That Monday, Spain and Portugal lost power for hours. Without electricity, cell towers lasted as long as their batteries did, and many phones were left with no data and no signal: no maps, no search, no way to look up what to do.

A blackout, a cyberattack, a storm or a massive IT failure is enough to silence your phone right when you need it most. Nomad Pocket keeps the essentials on the phone itself, so it keeps working when everything else fails.

Blackout

Without power, cell towers don’t last long. Your maps, guides and plan stay on your phone.

Internet outage

Your carrier or a big cloud service fails. Nothing you carry depends on them.

Cyberattack

If the networks or services you use are attacked, what’s yours stays on your phone, intact.

Storm or flood

When the weather takes the network down, you know where the shelter, water or hospital is.

No signal

Mountains, sea, a remote road: routes and answers without a single bar of signal.

Travelling

Abroad with no roaming or local SIM, with the local map and emergency numbers.

Maps and navigation

Maps that don’t switch off

Your whole country on your phone: every street, path and building. Get routes by car, on foot or by bike with no network, with spoken directions that keep guiding you with the screen locked.

  • Search any street or house number with no internet
  • Speed warning with each road’s limit
  • Compass, coordinates and UTM, saved places
  • Almost every country in the world, plus a built-in world map

CarPlay

Your route on your car’s screen

Connect your iPhone and Nomad Pocket shows up in CarPlay: the offline map and turn-by-turn navigation on your car’s screen, with spoken directions. Even with no signal, out on the open road.

  • Search for a destination, pick a saved place or the nearest hospital, pharmacy or fuel station
  • Maneuvers, distance and arrival time in the driver’s view
  • Start the trip on the phone or in the car: both stay in step

CarPlay: on iPhone.

Emergency

What you need, all around you

Hospitals, pharmacies, defibrillators, police, fire stations, drinking water and shelters, sorted by distance and direction. One tap and it takes you there.

  • Call your country’s emergency number
  • Share your exact position by message (coordinates and UTM)
  • GPS needs no data: you know where you are even with no signal

Emergency guide

What to do, step by step

200+ cards based on official sources: CPR, bleeding, burns, choking, fires, floods… In the app from the first minute, nothing to download.

  • Short texts, written to be read in a hurry
  • Every card cites its source
  • Instant search inside the guide

Offline assistant

An AI that doesn’t need the internet

Ask “how do I make water safe to drink?” in the middle of nowhere: it answers from your library and shows its sources. It runs on your phone, with Apple Intelligence or an open model you download.

  • Dictate your questions: your voice is processed on the device
  • Neither your questions nor its answers leave your phone

03 / How it works

Ready in a minute

Set it up today, while you’re online. Tomorrow you won’t need to be.

  1. 01

    Pick your country

    The app proposes a kit sized for your country and your phone’s storage: map and routes, guides, Wikipedia and the assistant.

  2. 02

    Download it on Wi‑Fi

    Leave it downloading. Home tells you how prepared you are and what’s missing, so you can finish while the network is up.

  3. 03

    Use it offline

    In airplane mode, in a blackout or in the middle of nowhere. And when your maps have newer versions, it lets you know.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. You only need a connection to download the content (Wi‑Fi is best) and to check for updates. After that, maps, routes, street search, emergency points, the guide, the library and the assistant all work in airplane mode.

Does GPS work without data?

Yes. GPS receives signals from satellites, not from the internet, so the map finds you even with no signal. The first fix may take a little longer than with data.

How much space does it take?

You decide. The welcome wizard suggests a kit for your country and free space (map and navigation, essential guides, Wikipedia and the assistant) and you can switch off each piece. A country takes from a few tens of MB to several GB.

Does it work with CarPlay?

Yes. Connect your iPhone to the car and Nomad Pocket shows up in CarPlay with your country’s map: search for destinations, find emergency services nearby and get spoken directions, all offline. Not on Android Auto yet.

Does it work in my country?

There are maps, routes and emergency points for almost every country in the world, and a world map is built in from the first second. The app is in English and Spanish, and the library has content in many languages.

Is it free? What’s the catch?

No catch: it’s free, with no ads, no purchases and no subscriptions. It’s an open source project with no server of its own, so there’s nothing to charge for and no data to sell.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or later. Android 8.0 or later, coming soon to Google Play. The assistant uses Apple Intelligence where available; otherwise you download a model in the app (from about 500 MB; larger ones need more memory).

What about Android?

It’s in review on Google Play and coming soon, with maps, routes, emergency services, the library, the plan and the assistant. First aid and medical information are only on iPhone and iPad.

Do you collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does it replace the emergency services?

No. It helps when there’s no network, but the AI can make mistakes and medical information is no substitute for a professional. If you can, always call the emergency number.
