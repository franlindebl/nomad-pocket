Built 2026-10-31 from inventories 1–4 only (no new research). One row per card in `cards.md`.
Note: `cards.md` says 95 cards, but it lists **94** (1.1–1.32, 2.1–2.5, 3.1–3.6, 4.1–4.7, 5.1–5.5, 6.1–6.2, 7.1–7.6,
8.1–8.4, 9.1–9.8, 10.1–10.6, 11.1–11.6, 12.1–12.6, 13.1).

**Keys.** EN: OK = current, reusable official English source; PARTIAL = exists but thin, outdated in part, or only a
fragment is usable; GAP = none. ES: SAME = official Spanish version from the same agency as the EN source; SPAIN =
Spanish-government source, different text; TRANSLATE = needs a faithful translation; GAP = nothing to copy or translate.
Flag: one per card (the most important), with a short detail. Abbreviations: ATP = ATP 4-02.11 (2026) in sections 1–2,
ATP 3-50.21 (2018) in section 12; MLP = MedlinePlus summaries; PC = Protección Civil (DGPCyE); NTP = INSST Nota
Técnica de Prevención.

## GAP cards

The five cards are GAP in both languages; no card is GAP in only one.

- **1.4 Choking, infant:** no current PD or reusable text; ATP has no infant technique, NTP 467 (1997) is outdated, WHO is NC-SA.
- **1.23 Poisoning (swallowed):** US sources only say "call Poison Control" (US-only); INTCF text can't be reused (only its phone number as a fact).
- **1.25 Drowning:** MedlinePlus covers prevention only; current rescue/resuscitation steps are only in ERC/AHA/Red Cross.
- **1.30 Emergency childbirth:** only a 1960s civil-defence booklet; no current lay guide in either language.
- **11.5 Toilets without running water:** no current household text; the CDC page is archived and aimed at responders (ATP 4-25.12 not opened).

Thin PARTIAL cards that are close to gaps: 1.2 (child/infant CPR: one paragraph), 1.7 (no step-by-step roll), 1.19 (no home ORS
recipe), 8.4 (no SOS / ground-to-air code beyond NPS whistle, flashlight and mirror), 3.5 (no pipe/heater draining steps),
4.7 (nothing on wild plants), 9.5 (no "never use water" line).
