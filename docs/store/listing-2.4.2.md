# App Store listing 2.4.2

2.4.2 (49) is 2.4.1 (47) plus the "Preparando… 99 %" fix, map search that weighs the match and the distance,
"Recientes" in the search, and CarPlay's "Terminar" as a map button. The version record was created through the App
Store Connect API on 2026-11-10 and inherited everything from 2.4.1 (name, subtitle, promotional text, description,
keywords, screenshots, App Review notes: `listing-2.4.0.md` and earlier). The only change is "What's new": the
owner asked for the full list with their own line, "Mejoras de visualización en Apple Car Play para el Konita", word
for word among the changes. es-MX = es-ES, en-GB = en-US, as before.

## What's new in 2.4.2

**es:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.4.2 mejora la búsqueda en el mapa y CarPlay.

• Los resultados de búsqueda tienen en cuenta lo cerca que están: lo que está a unas calles aparece entre los primeros
• Recientes: los sitios que elegiste en la búsqueda, a mano al abrirla
• Mejoras de visualización en Apple Car Play para el Konita
• CarPlay: el botón para terminar el viaje, ahora sobre el mapa
• Arreglado: la preparación de un país ya no se queda en el 99 %
```

**en:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.4.2 improves map search and CarPlay.

• Search results take distance into account: what's a few streets away comes among the first
• Recent: the places you picked in search, at hand when you open it
• Display improvements in Apple CarPlay for the Konita
• CarPlay: the button to end the trip, now on the map
• Fixed: preparing a country no longer stays at 99%
```
