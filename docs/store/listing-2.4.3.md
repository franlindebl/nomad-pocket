# App Store listing 2.4.3

2.4.3 (50) is 2.4.2 (49) plus Spain's official health points (pharmacies, botiquines, health centres,
consultorios and hospitals from the Ministry of Health's and the regions' registers, where OpenStreetMap lacks
them: `docs/OFFICIAL_POINTS.md`) and units that follow the country picked in the app, with a Units setting.
Everything is inherited from 2.4.2 but "What's new", approved by the owner on 2026-11-11.
es-MX = es-ES, en-GB = en-US, as before.

## What's new in 2.4.3

**es:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.4.3 completa el mapa de España con datos oficiales.

• España: miles de farmacias, botiquines, centros de salud, consultorios y hospitales que faltaban en el mapa, sacados de los registros oficiales del Ministerio de Sanidad y de las comunidades autónomas. Actualiza el mapa de España para verlos
• Las unidades (litros, kilómetros, km/h) siguen el país que elegiste en la app, y puedes cambiarlas en Ajustes > General > Unidades
```

**en:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.4.3 completes the map of Spain with official data.

• Spain: thousands of pharmacies, health centres, clinics and hospitals that were missing from the map, from the official registers of the Ministry of Health and the regions. Update the map of Spain to see them
• Units (litres, kilometres, km/h) follow the country you picked in the app, and you can change them in Settings > General > Units
```
