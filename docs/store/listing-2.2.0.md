## What's new in 2.2.0

**es:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.2 llega a CarPlay y prepara tu kit en mucho menos espacio.

• CarPlay: el mapa sin conexión y la navegación giro a giro en la pantalla del coche, con indicaciones por voz
• Desde el coche: busca un destino, elige un lugar guardado o el hospital, farmacia o gasolinera más cercanos
• El kit inicial descarga lo esencial de cada colección: primeros auxilios, supervivencia, huerto, reparaciones y libros de texto
• Wikipedia: los 50.000 mejores artículos completos (1,2 GB) en lugar de 13 GB. La completa sigue en la Biblioteca
• Nada se descarga dos veces: las guías que ya están dentro de otra no se vuelven a bajar
• Nueva enciclopedia de huerto y cultivo
```

**en:**

```
Nomad Pocket 2.2 comes to CarPlay and sets up your kit in far less space.

• CarPlay: the offline map and turn-by-turn navigation on your car's screen, with spoken directions
• From the car: search for a destination, pick a saved place or the nearest hospital, pharmacy or fuel station
• The starter kit downloads the essentials of every collection: first aid, survival, gardening, repairs and textbooks
• Wikipedia: its 50,000 best articles in full (1.2 GB) instead of 13 GB. The whole one is still in the Library
• Nothing downloads twice: guides already inside another one aren't fetched again
• A new gardening and growing encyclopedia
```

## App Review notes

The 2.0.0 notes (`listing-2.0.0.md`), with "Background modes (new in this version)" reworded to "Background modes"
and this paragraph after it:

```
CarPlay (new in this version, navigation app entitlement, Case-ID 23304795): the CarPlay scene shows the offline map following the driver, a destination list (offline search of streets and places, saved places, and "Emergency nearby": the nearest hospitals, pharmacies, police and fuel), a trip preview with the route's length and time, and turn-by-turn guidance with maneuvers, arrival estimates and voice prompts. Routes are calculated on the device; nothing is sent. To try it: on the phone, download Andorra (Maps), then connect to CarPlay (or the CarPlay Simulator) and open Nomad Pocket; from Destinations pick a saved place, a search result or an emergency point, then "Go".
```
