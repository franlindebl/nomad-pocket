1. **Don't give substitutes.** A baby under 6 months must never have: cow's, goat's or any other animal milk, plant drinks (soya, oat, almond, rice), homemade formula, sugar water, juice or herbal teas. They lack nutrients, strain the baby's kidneys and can make the baby very ill.
2. **Don't stretch it with extra water.** Always make it up with the exact measure on the pack. With too much water the baby isn't fed; with too little the baby gets dehydrated.
3. **Another brand will do.** If you can't find the usual one, give another first infant milk (the "1"). If the baby has a special formula (hydrolysed, for allergy, or for premature babies), ask the paediatrician or health centre first.
4. **Where to get it:** pharmacies (including the ones on duty at night), supermarkets, your health centre, the emergency shelter or aid point, Cruz Roja (Red Cross) and the town hall's social services. If there is no safe water, ready-to-feed liquid formula is better.
5. **If there is none anywhere and the baby is going to miss feeds, call 112.**

## If you breastfeed or used to

6. Put the baby to the breast often, skin to skin, even if little milk comes. Your supply can grow, and even come back if you stopped weeks or months ago (relactation). Meanwhile, keep giving formula if you have it. Ask your health centre for help.
7. Don't give breast milk bought online or from strangers: it can pass on infections or be contaminated. In Spain, milk banks supply hospitals for newborns who are admitted.

## Food is no substitute

8. Under 6 months (at 5 months, say), purées and baby cereal don't replace milk. Other foods start at 6 months.
9. **From 6 to 12 months:** cow's milk isn't normal before the first birthday either. Only as a last resort, for a short time and ideally with a health worker's go-ahead, whole UHT cow's milk. Never raw, unpasteurised milk.

**Call 112** if the baby wets far fewer nappies, is listless or very sleepy, or won't feed.
