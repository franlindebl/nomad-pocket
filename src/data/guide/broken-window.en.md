1. **Keep away from the glass** and keep children and pets out. During the storm stay away from the window; if you live on an upper floor, beware of glass that could fall into the street. Put on sturdy shoes and thick gloves before you go near it.
2. **Photograph the damage before covering or cleaning**, for the insurer. Contact your insurer.
3. **Carefully remove loose pieces** that could fall. Leave large pieces that are still firm in the frame.
4. **Cover the opening** with a board (exterior plywood), thick cardboard or thick plastic, and fix it to the frame or wall with screws, nails or strong tape. If it is wood, fix it to the structure, not just the cladding.
5. **Tape stuck on glass does not stop it breaking**: it helps cover an opening, it does not strengthen a window.
6. **Clear the glass** with a brush and dustpan, and the small pieces with damp kitchen paper. Throw it away wrapped in thick card or paper.
7. **If you cut yourself**, press on the wound with a clean cloth.
