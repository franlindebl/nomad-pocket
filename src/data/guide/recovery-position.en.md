What should you do if the person is unconscious, or not awake and able to respond? If the injured person is breathing, move them into the recovery position. If they aren’t breathing, and you are able, remove obstructions and perform CPR.

The recovery position is a way to place the body on its side to keep the airway open and prevent choking.

The recovery position allows blood and mucus to drain out of the casualty’s nose and mouth and not drain back into the airway. The recovery position also helps to protect against inhaling vomit if the casualty throws up.

**WARNING**

If the casualty is lying on their chest (prone position), cautiously roll the casualty as a unit so that their body does not twist. The twisting may further complicate a back, neck, or spinal injury.
