**Use it** if the person doesn't respond, or barely responds, but **is breathing normally**.

**Don't use it** if they aren't breathing normally (gasping, or slow and laboured breathing): start CPR. Don't use it either if they have had a hard knock or an injury: leave them face up.

1. **Call** the emergency number, or have someone call, with the speaker on.
2. Kneel beside them. Make sure both their legs are straight.
3. Place the arm nearest to you at a right angle to their body, straight or with the elbow bent.
4. Bring their other arm across their chest and hold the back of their hand against their cheek nearest to you. Keep it there.
5. With your other hand, grasp the far leg just above the knee and lift it to bend the knee, keeping the foot on the ground.
6. Keeping their hand against the cheek, pull on that bent leg to roll them gently towards you onto their side.
7. Adjust the upper leg so that the hip and knee are bent at right angles.
8. Gently tilt their head back so the airway stays open. Adjust the hand under the cheek so the head stays tilted and facing the ground: this lets anything in the mouth drain out.
9. **If she is clearly pregnant**, place her on her left side.

## While you wait

10. **Check often that they are still breathing normally.** For a child, check continuously or at least every minute.
11. **If they stop breathing normally**, or you are in doubt, turn them onto their back and start CPR.
12. Don't leave them alone, except to go for help.
13. If they stay like this for 30 minutes, roll them onto the other side.
