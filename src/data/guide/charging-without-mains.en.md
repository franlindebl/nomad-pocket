## Power bank

- It is the simplest option. Keep it charged and top it up now and then even if you don't use it.
- Its capacity is given in mAh. A 10,000 mAh one gives about 2 charges of an ordinary phone: some energy is lost while charging, and big phones use more. What really counts is Wh (watt hours): mAh × volts ÷ 1000.
- For an outage of a day or two, one big power bank is enough. If the outage may last more than a few days, a solar panel lets you recharge it.

## Small solar panel

1. Face it towards the sun, with no shade on it, and turn it as the sun moves.
2. With clouds it gives much less energy: expect it to charge little.
3. Better to charge a power bank with it, and the phone from the power bank: with clouds the current rises and falls and the phone keeps stopping the charge.
4. Keep the phone and the power bank in the shade: they charge worse when hot.

## In the car

- Charge with the engine running and the car outdoors. Never with the engine on inside a garage, not even with the door open: carbon monoxide kills.
- With the engine off, don't charge for long: you can drain the car battery and be unable to start it.

## Hand-crank charger

It gives little energy and takes effort: enough for a call or a few messages, not to charge the phone fully.

## Make the battery last

- Turn the screen brightness down.
- Turn on low power or battery saver mode.
- Use airplane mode when you don't need signal; with weak signal the phone uses more searching for it.
- Close apps and turn off wifi, Bluetooth and location if you don't use them.
- Don't use the phone as a torch: it uses a lot of battery.
- Send texts instead of calling, and switch it off if you won't use it for hours.
