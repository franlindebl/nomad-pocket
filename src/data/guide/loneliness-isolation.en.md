Spending days without seeing anyone (because of snow, a blackout, a flood or a lockdown) can bring sadness, fear and anxiety. What helps:

1. **Stay in touch.** Every day, send a message or make a short call to someone you trust; during a power cut, messages get through better than calls, and keeping it short saves battery. Tell them how you feel.
2. **Check on your neighbours**, especially anyone who lives alone or is older: a message or a call is enough. Helping others helps you too.
3. **Keep a routine**: get up, eat and go to bed at set times, do some exercise at home and your daily chores.
4. **Do things you enjoy and find relaxing**, such as singing, painting or other activities.
5. **Look after your body**: sleep, eat healthily and avoid alcohol and other drugs.
6. **Stay informed without overload**: news from official sources, once or twice a day.
7. **Ask for help if you need it.** Teléfono de la Esperanza (717 003 717) answers at any hour. If you are thinking of ending your life, call 024 (free, 24 hours). In an emergency, call 112.

If sadness or fear stops you getting on with daily life for several days in a row, contact your health centre.
