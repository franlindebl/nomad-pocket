1. **Stay calm. Don't run and don't shout.** Wild boar are not usually aggressive towards people and tend to run away. They only attack if they are hurt or feel threatened.
2. **Don't approach or touch them.** They are wild animals and can react unpredictably: there is a risk of being bitten or rammed.
3. **Put the dog on a lead and keep it with you.** A loose dog can provoke a clash.
4. **Move away calmly** and let the animal go. Don't corner them, don't get between a sow and her young, and leave them an escape route. Don't turn your back on them.
5. **Never feed them.** At a picnic spot, put your food away: they can snatch it aggressively. Close rubbish bags well and keep your pets' food out of their reach.
6. **If it charges**, get behind a tree or a car and protect your legs.
7. **If you see one in a town or village**, tell the local police or the town council. Call 112 only if there is danger.
8. **If you are in a car**, drive carefully: they can cross the road and cause a collision.
9. **If you are bitten**, wash the wound with soap and water and see a doctor (risk of tetanus and infection).
