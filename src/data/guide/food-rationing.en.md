## At home

1. **Take stock.** Count what you have and divide it between the people and the days you want it to last. Use first what goes off first.
2. **Fridge food first, then the freezer, and dry food and tins last.** Open the freezer as little as possible.
3. **Cut back on food, never on water.** Drink what you need today and look for more for tomorrow. To need less water, move little and keep cool.
4. **Cut back, but don't stop eating:** have at least one full meal a day. A healthy adult who moves little can live for a long time on half of what they normally eat.
5. **Don't cut back for children or pregnant women.** Take special care of babies, older people and the sick. Anyone breastfeeding should eat and drink as much as they can, though they can keep breastfeeding if they go hungry.
6. **What feeds most for little money:** rice, pasta, pulses and oil. Fat gives more than twice the energy of the rest. As a guide, an adult needs about 2,000 kcal a day to function at a minimum level; the label gives the kcal in each pack. In the cold you need more.
7. **If water is short**, avoid salty and very high-protein food (cured meats, dried meat): it makes you thirsty and needs more water to digest. Choose tins with plenty of liquid.

## Lost outdoors

8. **Water comes first.** People can live for several weeks without food, though with less and less strength and a duller mind.
9. **Save sweat, not water:** rest in the shade in the hottest hours and avoid any effort that doesn't help you get out or be found.
10. **Ration the food you carry** for the days it may take. If you have less than a litre of water a day, don't eat salty, dry or very high-protein food.
11. **Don't eat any plant you haven't identified for sure**, and never mushrooms: some kill and no home test can tell.
