1. **Stop for a second and look** where the problem is and where there are fewer people. Move there calmly, away from the mass of people. If people are fleeing an attacker or an explosion, away from that threat.
2. **Leave as soon as you feel uncomfortable**, while you can still move. The longer you wait, the harder it gets.
3. **Go to the sides** of the flow of people, not the middle, and to the nearest exit you can reach, emergency exits included. Many people head for the door they came in by, and that is where the jam forms.
4. **Don't stop** in doorways, aisles or walkways. Keep away from walls, fences, pillars and barriers: the crowd presses hardest against them.
5. **Danger signs:** if people touch you on every side, you feel pushing in waves, or you can't lift your hands to your face, get out now.

## If you are already caught

6. **Stay on your feet.** If someone falls, they bring down the people around them.
7. **Arms bent in front of your chest**, like a boxer, to keep room to breathe.
8. **Don't push against the crowd:** let the surge carry you while keeping your balance and, between surges, move sideways, a little at a time.
9. **Save your breath:** don't shout unless you must, and breathe calmly.
10. **If you fall, get up at once:** on the ground, the weight of others can pin you down.
11. **Help others:** call back "stop!" or "slow down!", and keep children by the hand or in your arms.
12. When you are safe, **call 112** and say where you are and what is happening.

## When you arrive somewhere crowded

Note where the exits are, emergency exits included, and agree on a meeting point in case you get separated.
