If the authorities order an evacuation, leave as soon as they say: your life is worth more than the house. These steps are for when the fire is coming and there is still time.

## Outside the house

1. Turn off the gas: butane or propane bottles, tank or meter. Move the gas barbecue and other gas appliances that are outside away from the house.
2. Bring inside, or move away from the house, things that burn easily: firewood, garden furniture, toys, doormats, rubbish bins.
3. If there is time, clear dry leaves, branches and dry grass from against the walls and off the roof.
4. Fill buckets and other large containers with water and leave them around the house. Connect the hoses to the outside taps.
5. Park the car in a clear spot facing the way out, with the windows closed and the keys at hand. Put the emergency bag in it.

## Inside the house

1. Move curtains and furniture that burn easily away from the windows.
2. Gather the pets in one room so you can find them quickly.
3. Charge your phone.
4. Put on cotton or wool clothes that cover your arms and legs, gloves and sturdy shoes.

## If you are told to evacuate

Lock the house, tell someone outside the area where you are going, turn on the car's lights and follow the route the authorities give.
