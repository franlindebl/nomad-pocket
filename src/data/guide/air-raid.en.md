If the air-raid siren sounds, the authorities warn of an attack (aircraft, missiles, drones) or you hear explosions nearby:

1. **Take cover now**, in the nearest shelter or protected place. Don't delay.
2. **Where, from most to least protection:** a civil defence shelter (sign: a blue triangle on an orange background); a basement, an underground car park or an underground metro station; inside a building, an inner room with no windows, or with as few outside walls and windows as possible, or the stairwell; a tunnel or next to a wall. Inside a building you are safer than outside.
3. **Away from windows:** explosions shatter glass and the shards injure. Sit on the floor, below window level, against an inner wall that isn't opposite a window.
4. **If you are on the top floor** and there is no protected room, go down to a lower floor and stay in the stairwell or an inner room.
5. **Outside:** go into the nearest building. If there is no time, lie down on the ground, ideally in a hollow or a ditch, and protect your head with your hands.
6. **In a car:** pull over, get out and go into a building. If there is no time, lie down on the ground outside the car and protect your head.
7. **Stay under cover** until the all-clear sounds or the authorities say you can come out. Listen to the radio and to official alerts on your phone.
8. If they warn of nuclear, chemical or biological weapons, take cover in the same way: a shelter gives the best protection.

## Afterwards

9. **Don't touch unknown objects or the remains of a missile or rocket.** Move away, keep onlookers back and tell the police.
10. If the building is damaged, check for fire, stay low if there is smoke, don't use the lift and avoid floors and stairs that look damaged.
11. **Help the injured** if it is safe. If someone is trapped under debris, don't move it: tell the emergency services where they are.
12. Let your family know with a message and keep phone calls for emergencies.

## Keep ready

- Where the nearest shelters are to your home, your work and the school.
- A battery radio, a torch, water and food for a few days, a first aid kit, medicines, documents, cash, your phone and charger, and warm clothes. Be prepared to spend a few days in a shelter.
