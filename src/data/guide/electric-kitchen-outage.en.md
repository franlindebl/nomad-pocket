1. **Without power,** ceramic and induction hobs, electric ovens and microwaves don't work.
2. **Don't cook indoors** with camping stoves, barbecues or braziers: carbon monoxide builds up and can kill without warning. A closed or glazed-in balcony counts as indoors. An open balcony next to the door or window is not a safe place either.
3. **Eat without cooking.** Shop-bought (commercial) tinned food can be eaten straight from the tin, without heating. Don't eat from swollen, dented or rusty tins or jars. Good choices are tinned pulses, tuna, sardines, meat and vegetables, tinned fruit, biscuits, cereal, nuts, dried fruit, cereal bars, peanut butter, UHT milk and juice cartons.
4. **Simple ideas:** tinned pulses with tuna; biscuits with sardines or peanut butter; cereal with UHT milk; fruit with nuts.
5. **Fridge:** keep the door shut. If the cut lasts no more than 4 hours, refrigerated food should be safe. Never taste food or trust its look or smell to judge if it is safe. What to throw away: see the fridge power-cut card.
6. **If you need to heat something,** use the camping stove or barbecue outdoors only, away from windows, doors and vents. To heat a tin: take the label off, wash and disinfect it with diluted bleach (1 part to 10 parts water), and open it before heating.
7. **For the next power cut:** keep several days' food that needs no fridge, cooking or water, and a manual tin opener.
