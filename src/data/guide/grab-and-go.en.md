People and animals first. Things, only those you have time to grab.

## What to take, in this order

1. **Your emergency bag**, if you have one ready: it already holds almost everything.
2. **Medicines** for each person and the list of what they take. **Glasses**, contact lenses, hearing aids and other aids.
3. **Documents:** ID card or passport and your important papers, if you keep them together. Important phone numbers and insurance details, written on paper.
4. **Phone, charger and power bank.**
5. **Cash and a card.** House and car keys.
6. **Water and something to eat.**
7. **Warm clothes and a waterproof.** Put on sturdy shoes, long trousers and long sleeves.
8. **Babies:** formula, bottles, nappies and wipes.
9. **Pets:** lead or carrier, food, water and their medicines. If you go, they go too.
10. If there's room: a torch and a battery radio.

## Before you leave

- If the house is damaged and the authorities tell you to, turn off the water, gas and electricity.
- Unplug small appliances. Leave the fridge and freezer plugged in, unless there is a risk of flooding.
- Lock doors and windows.
- Leave a note saying when you left and where you are going.
- If there's time, tell your contact outside the area where you are going.
- Ask your neighbours if anyone needs a lift.
- Follow the routes the authorities give; don't take shortcuts.
