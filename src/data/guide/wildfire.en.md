## Near a fire

Call 112 urgently.

- If the fire is very small and just starting, try to put it out, always putting your own safety first.
- If the fire is bigger, move away in the direction opposite to the smoke. Breathe through your nose, trying to cover it with a wet cloth.
- Do not seek shelter in deep hollows, and always flee downhill.
- Do not try to cross through the flames, you could get trapped. If there is no other way out, cross where the fire front is weakest.
- If the fire reaches you, get onto ground that has already burned, always with your back to the prevailing wind.
- If your clothes catch fire, do not run: roll on the ground and, if you have a blanket, cover yourself with it.

## Fire near a country house

- Do not throw water on areas where there may be electric cables.
- Use hoses to wet the roof and the surroundings.
- Go inside the house and close all doors, windows and shutters to prevent possible draughts and flames getting in.
- (*) If you receive an evacuation order, follow the instructions of the relevant authority.
- Disconnect all utilities.
- Seal the gaps to the outside with cloths soaked in water.

(*) Unless the authorities have ordered an evacuation, it is advisable not to leave the house.
