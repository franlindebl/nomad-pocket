## Protect yourself

1. **Children, older people, pregnant women and the ill** (asthma…) should stay out of the clean-up. Keep children away from the mud.
2. **Rubber boots, long clothes, gloves, a mask and goggles.** Change and wash clothes daily, separately, in hot water.
3. **Cover cuts and scratches** with waterproof plasters. Wash any wound that gets wet with soap and water. If a wound is deep or dirty, or you don't know when you last had a tetanus jab, ask at the health centre.
4. **Take your gloves off before eating**, using your phone or touching your face, and wash your hands with clean water and soap.

## Clean and dry

5. **Air the place.** Clean walls and floors with soap and water, then disinfect with water and bleach. Never mix it with other products.
6. **Spanish bleach is often weaker** than US bleach (35–60 g of active chlorine per litre): follow the disinfecting dose on its label.
7. **Against mould**, dry the house within 24–48 hours if you can, with doors and windows open. Throw away what you can't clean and dry quickly: mattresses, rugs, upholstered sofas, soft toys, books and papers.

## Rats and mice

8. **Don't touch them**, alive or dead, without gloves. A dead rat goes in two sealed bags in the rubbish.
9. **Don't sweep or vacuum their droppings.** Soak them with bleach diluted for disinfecting (the dose on its label), wait 5 minutes, pick them up with kitchen paper and bin it, bagged.
10. **Block holes**, keep food and water in closed containers and take the rubbish out soon.
11. **Use snap traps**, out of reach of children and pets. Poison only if they keep coming, and as the label says. If there are many, tell the town council.

## Fever or diarrhoea after the mud

12. Fever, muscle aches, headache, vomiting or diarrhoea in the next month may be an infection from the water or mud, such as leptospirosis (from rat urine). **See a doctor and say they were in the water or mud.** Meanwhile, they should drink plenty.
13. **A&E or 112** if they are very weak or drowsy, have yellow skin or eyes, hardly pass urine or find it hard to breathe.
