**Always work out the dose from the child's weight, not their age.** If you don't know their weight, weigh yourself holding the child and subtract your own weight.

## Paracetamol

1. **15 mg per kilo** per dose, every 6 hours, no more than 4 doses a day.
2. **Example, 12 kg:** 12 × 15 = 180 mg. With a 100 mg/ml syrup (such as Apiretal) that is **1.8 ml**. Shortcut for 100 mg/ml only: kilos × 0.15 = ml.

## Ibuprofen

3. **Never under 3 months of age or under 5 kg.** Under 2 years or with asthma, only if a doctor has said so.
4. **20 to 30 mg per kilo a day**, in 3 doses (every 8 hours). Never more than 30 mg per kilo a day.
5. **Example, 12 kg:** 80 to 120 mg per dose: **4 to 6 ml** of Dalsy 20 mg/ml or **2 to 3 ml** of Dalsy 40 mg/ml.
6. Don't give it if the child is dehydrated (vomiting, diarrhoea or drinking little) or has chickenpox.

## To avoid mistakes

- Check the **mg per ml** on the box: the same ml of another brand or strength is not the same dose.
- Use the syringe or dropper from that bottle, never a spoon. Don't mix up ml and drops.
- Write down the time and amount of each dose.
- Don't give both at once or alternate them unless a doctor tells you to.
- **Never give aspirin** to anyone under 16, nor medicines made for adults.

## Fever when you can't reach the paediatrician

- Give drinks often; if breastfed, keep breastfeeding. Don't wrap them up too much or wet them with cold water.
- The medicine is to make them more comfortable, not to bring the fever down at all costs.
- **Call 112 or go to A&E (urgencias)** if they are under 3 months with 38 °C or more, have trouble breathing, are very drowsy or not responding, have a seizure, a stiff neck, spots that don't fade when you press a glass on them, or are dehydrated (nappy almost dry, crying without tears). If the fever lasts more than 3 days, have a doctor see them.
- **If you gave too much**, even if they seem fine: Spain's Toxicology Information Service, 91 562 04 20, or A&E.
