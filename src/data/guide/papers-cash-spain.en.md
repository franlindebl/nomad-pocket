## Papers to keep at hand

Keep them together in a waterproof sleeve or bag, easy to grab if you have to leave home:

- DNI or NIE and passport for everyone in the household, and the driving licence.
- Everyone's health card (tarjeta sanitaria).
- The family book (libro de familia).
- A list of the medicines each person takes, with the dose, and any important medical reports.
- Insurance policies (home, car, life, health) and each insurer's phone number.
- The deeds to your home or your rental contract.
- Important phone numbers written on paper: family, doctor, bank (to block cards) and insurers.

## Copies

- Take photos or scans of all these papers and keep them on your phone, on a USB stick inside the kit and, if you can, in the cloud.
- Also carry paper photocopies of the most important ones, in case your phone runs out of battery.

## Cash

- Keep some cash at home, somewhere safe: in a power cut, cards, card machines and cash machines can stop working.
- Small notes and some coins are best.
- How much? There is no official figure; the advice is a small amount.
