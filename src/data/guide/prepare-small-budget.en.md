Getting prepared doesn't have to cost much. Start with what's free and buy a little at a time. First goal: being able to manage at home for at least 72 hours (3 days) without help.

## 1. Free, today

- Think about which emergencies are most likely where you live.
- Make a family plan: how you'll reach each other, a meeting point and where you'd go if you have to leave home.
- Write important phone numbers on paper, not only in your phone.
- Keep copies of important documents in a sealed plastic bag, somewhere high up, and another copy in digital form.
- You don't need to sign up for anything to get warnings: ES-Alert reaches phones in the area on its own, with no registration or app.
- Look for free or discounted first aid courses near you.

## 2. Water

Store tap water in clean food-grade containers with tight lids, such as washed water bottles. Write the date on them and change the water every 6 months. Goal: one gallon (almost 4 litres) per person per day, for at least 3 days.

## 3. Food

Each time you go shopping, pick up one extra item you already eat that keeps without a fridge, ideally on offer. Keep going until you have enough for at least 3 days. Use the oldest first and replace it.

## 4. Light and news

A torch, spare batteries and a battery or wind-up radio. Check first what you already have at home.

## 5. First aid kit and medicines

A basic first aid kit. If someone takes medicines every day, organise them and keep them protected for an emergency.

## Spending less

- Use what you already have: blankets, old clothes that still fit, backpacks, kitchen things.
- Look in second-hand shops, flea markets and sales: camping gear, tools, cookware.
- Check the kit once a year and replace anything out of date.
