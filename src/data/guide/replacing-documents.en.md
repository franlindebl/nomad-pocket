## Lost or stolen wallet

1. **Call your bank now** (or your card's phone line) to block the cards.
2. **Report the loss or theft**; in Spain, at a Policía Nacional station (091) or a Guardia Civil post (062). Keep a copy of the report.
3. **Book an appointment** (cita previa) at a DNI issuing office to get a new one. If you don't bring the report, the officer makes an administrative report before issuing it.
4. **If your DNI has an electronic certificate**, go to the office to cancel it too, so that nobody can use it online.
5. Meanwhile, use any copies you have (photos or scans).

## Soaked papers

1. **Act soon:** after about 48 hours mould can start.
2. **Be careful:** wet paper is heavy and tears easily. Hold it fully supported, on a flat, firm surface.
3. **If it is safe** (electricity), take the papers out of the water and gently drain the excess. If the water was dirty (flood, sewage), wear gloves and wash your hands well afterwards.
4. **To air dry them**, lay them on clean towels or unprinted absorbent paper, in a cool place with moving air. Don't point a fan straight at the papers.
5. **If there are many**, or you can't dry them now, put them in the freezer: it stops the damage and the mould until you can dry them properly. Take photos first.
6. **Copies:** ask the notary for a duplicate of the deeds or check with the Registro de la Propiedad, and ask your bank branch for the bank papers.
