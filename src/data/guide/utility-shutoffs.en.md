Find the three shut-offs **now**, before an emergency, and show everyone in the family where they are.

## Where they usually are

- **Water:** the stopcock (llave de paso), in the kitchen, in the bathroom or by the meter, at the entrance or in the building's hallway. Turn it clockwise until it closes.
- **Power:** the electrical panel, usually near the front door. Switch off the main switch (ICP). Switch off the other breakers too.
- **Gas:** the valve for the home, by the meter or in the kitchen, or the valve on the bottle. For the home valve, turn a quarter turn until it is crosswise to the pipe. For the bottle, turn the regulator clockwise until it stops.

## When to shut off

- **Gas:** if you smell gas (then do not touch the electrical panel or any switch: close only the gas, open windows and leave), or after an earthquake or an explosion if there is damage. See the gas leak card.
- **Water:** if a pipe has burst or the house is flooding.
- **Power:** if there is water in the house, damaged wires or sparks, but only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water or touching anything wet.
- **If you are told to evacuate** and there is time, shut off all three before leaving, but the power only if there is no smell of gas.

## Getting back to normal

- **Gas:** only the gas company or an approved installer may check the installation and turn it back on.
- **Power:** turn the main switch on first, then the other breakers one at a time. If it trips again or there is water, call an electrician.
- **Water:** open the valve slowly and check for leaks.
