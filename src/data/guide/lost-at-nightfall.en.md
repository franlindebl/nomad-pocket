1. **Stop and stay where you are.** If you can't retrace your steps safely, don't go on: changing location makes it harder for rescuers to find you. Move only if your life is in immediate danger.
2. **Call for help.** Call 112 (or 062, Guardia Civil). Say what happened, where you are (your phone's GPS coordinates, if you have them), how you all are, and what clothes and gear you have.
3. **Save battery.** Keep your phone for talking to rescuers and use your torch only when you need it.
4. **Take shelter.** Get out of the wind and rain: rocks, bushes, a fallen tree. Put on all the clothes you have, do them up and cover your head; if you have a space blanket, use it.
5. **Get off the ground.** At rest you lose a lot of heat to the ground: sit or lie on dry leaves, pine needles or branches.
6. **Be seen and heard.** Blow your whistle three times, pause, and three times again. Put on your reflective vest or your brightest clothes. At night, flash your torch towards rescuers when you see or hear them.
7. **Don't light a fire** if there is a risk of wildfire or it is wildfire season.
8. **Be patient.** Rescue can take hours or even days.

## If you see the helicopter

- Stand in an open spot where you can be seen clearly from the air.
- **Both arms raised in a Y: "Yes, I need help".**
- One arm up and the other down, on a diagonal: "I don't need help".
- Make the signal slowly and repeat it until you see they have understood.
- If it is going to land, pick up anything that could blow away and get caught in the rotor.
