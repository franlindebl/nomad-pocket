## Electrical burns

Electric current can cause injuries, above all as it passes through the inside of the body. The effects of an electrical accident on our body can trigger cardio-respiratory arrest, tetanic contractions, seizures… Locally, electricity can cause skin burns at the points of entry and exit. The priority, as in all accidents, will be P.A.S. (Protect - Alert - Help)

The course of action will be:

1. Cut off the current, under safe conditions, but not before anticipating the person's fall.
2. Begin the primary assessment and, in case of cardio-respiratory arrest, begin basic life support.
3. Look for other possible injuries such as bleeding, shock, fractures. The most serious injury will always be treated first.
4. Put a clean, sterile dressing on the burns
5. Evacuate, under medical supervision and urgently, the worker who has suffered an electric shock, even if they show no ill effects.
