Do not build a fire if there is a high fire risk at the park or if it is wildland fire season. It could cause a wildfire and put you and others at great risk.

## Preparing a fire site

- Clear the ground of fallen leaves, vegetation, and duff; expose 3 feet (1 meter) in diameter of soil at a minimum to prevent the fire from spreading along the ground.
- Porous rock is removed from the fire site, which is at risk of exploding from the heat of the fire.

## Building a fire

The 3 phases are tinder, kindling, and fuel, each representing an increase in fuel size appropriate for heat and oxygen available to support it.

**Tinder.** A dry material can be ignited by a small spark, coal, or flame. Natural tinder examples include—

- Dead dry grass, ferns, moss, lichen, and fungi.
- Fine wood scrapings or shavings (heartwood preferred).

**Kindling.** Kindling is added once the tinder is burning strong and mostly engulfed by flame. It should be laid over the tinder fire so that some spaces are left in the kindling for the tinder fire to burn through.

**Fuel.** Wood used as fuel should be the diameter of the forearm at a minimum. Effective fuel sources include dry, standing, dead wood and dry, dead branches (those that snap when broken).

**Caution.** Highly flammable liquids should not be poured on an existing fire. Even a smoldering fire can cause liquids to explode and cause serious burns.

## Lighting it

Fires are started from the upwind side to protect the fragile fire-starting heat source. Once the match head is ignited, it is held with the match head pointing down at an angle so the flame can begin to climb the matchstick and gain strength. It is critical that the flame is protected from the wind during this step. Once the initial flame gains strength, the match is held near the bottom edge of tinder to ignite it.

## Putting it out

Fires with nonpetroleum-based fuel can be extinguished with an adequate amount of water.

**Caution.** When leaving an area after having a fire, ensure that the ground under where the fire was built is cool to the touch.
