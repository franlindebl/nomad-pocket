1. **Keep a reserve of 7 to 10 days** of every medicine taken daily, on top of the box in use.
2. **Talk to your family doctor or pharmacist** about building up that reserve with your e-prescription without going beyond what is prescribed. With your health card (tarjeta sanitaria) you can collect your e-prescription at any pharmacy in Spain.
3. **Rotate the reserve:** always use the oldest box first and put the new one behind it. Check expiry dates: an expired medicine may not work or may not be safe.
4. **Store it well:** in its box, with the leaflet, somewhere cool and dry, in a container that closes well and keeps water out, and out of sight and reach of children and pets.
5. **Medicines kept in the fridge** (such as insulin): have a cool box and ice packs for a power cut.
6. **Keep this information on paper and on your phone:**
   - a list with the name of each medicine and its active ingredient, the dose, the times and what it is for;
   - allergies and the supplies needed (glucose strips, needles…);
   - the treatment sheet and the latest medical report, and a copy of the prescription.
7. **Don't suddenly stop** blood-pressure or heart tablets or change the dose to save them: ask the doctor first.
8. **If you look after someone else** (your mother, for example), keep their list and reserve too, and make sure someone else knows where they are.
