## If you are inside

1. **Stay calm**: there is air inside and the lift will not fall. Press the alarm button.
2. **Use the intercom** if there is one, to say where you are and what is happening.
3. **Reassure the people with you.**
4. **Wait and don't tamper with the lift.** Don't force the doors or try to get out: you put yourself in danger, above all if the cabin is between two floors or if the lift starts moving again.
5. **If the alarm doesn't work or there is danger**, call the emergency number posted in the cabin, or 112. Say which building you are in and which floor.
6. If someone feels unwell, tell 112.

## If you are outside and someone is inside

1. Talk to the person, tell them to stay calm and not to try to get out.
2. Call the lift maintenance company and, if there is danger or nobody answers, 112.
3. **Don't try to open the doors** to get them out: wait for someone who knows how.

In a fire or a power cut, don't use lifts: take the stairs.
