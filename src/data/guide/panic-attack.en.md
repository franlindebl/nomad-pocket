A panic attack comes on suddenly: your heart races, you are short of breath or feel you are choking, you sweat, shake, feel dizzy, feel tingling in your fingers or lips, and fear losing control or dying. It is very frightening, but it is not dangerous and it passes: it usually lasts 5 to 20 minutes.

## First, think of a heart attack

**Call 112** if there is:

- chest pain that feels tight or squeezing, or that spreads to the arms, neck or jaw;
- severe difficulty breathing: gasping or unable to get words out;
- pale, blue or grey lips or skin;
- someone who has passed out and is not responding normally.

Many panic symptoms can be caused by other problems. If you are not sure it is anxiety, or another symptom worries you, see a doctor.

## During the attack

1. Stay where you are, if you can, sitting or leaning on something.
2. Breathe slowly: breathe in through your nose counting from 1 to 5 and out through your mouth counting from 1 to 5. Keep going for at least 5 minutes.
3. Remind yourself that it will pass and that it is not life-threatening.
4. Think of calm, pleasant images.
5. Tell someone you trust how you feel. You are not alone: almost everyone feels fear or anxiety at some point.

## If you can't sleep

- Go to bed and get up at similar times each day.
- Put your phone and screens away an hour before sleep, and don't spend the day watching the news.
- Find a quiet, dark and cool place.
- Write down your worries before going to bed.
- Avoid coffee, tea and alcohol before sleep.
- If you are still awake after 20 minutes, get up, do something calm and go back to bed when you feel sleepy.

## If you are afraid of giving birth alone

If you go into labour and can't get to hospital, call 112: they can guide you by phone through the birth. Ask someone you trust to be with you.

## Help

- If the anxiety doesn't go away or you can't cope with it, tell your doctor or your health centre.
- Teléfono de la Esperanza: 717 003 717, at any hour.
- If you are thinking of ending your life: 024, free, confidential, 24 hours.
