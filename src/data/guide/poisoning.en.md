1. **If the person doesn't respond and isn't breathing normally**, call the emergency number and start CPR. If there is some of the substance around their mouth, don't give mouth-to-mouth: give compressions only, without stopping.
2. **If the person doesn't respond but is breathing**, put them in the recovery position and check often that they are still breathing.
3. **Call the emergency number** if you think they have swallowed something harmful, even if they feel fine: symptoms can take hours or days to appear. Call straight away if they have great difficulty breathing or have a seizure.
4. **If you don't know whether what they swallowed is dangerous**, in Spain call the Toxicology Information Service (Servicio de Información Toxicológica): 91 562 04 20, 24 hours a day.

## What not to do

- **Don't make them vomit.** They could choke and stop breathing.
- **Don't give them anything to eat or drink**, not even water or milk.

## While help is on the way

- If they are awake, stay with them and keep talking to them so you notice if they lose consciousness.
- Look for what they may have taken: packaging, bottles, blister packs, medicines.
- Keep the packaging or a little of the substance to show the healthcare team.
- If they vomit, keep a little of the vomit in a container for the healthcare team.
- Protect yourself: avoid touching the substance with your skin.
