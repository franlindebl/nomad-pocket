Besides the basic kit (plasters, gauze, tape, antiseptic, gloves and scissors), it's worth keeping at home:

## Supplies

- A thermometer.
- Saline solution, to wash wounds, burns and the eyes.
- Bandages.
- Tweezers.
- Face masks.
- A list of emergency phone numbers: 112 and Spain's Toxicology Information Service (Servicio de Información Toxicológica), 91 562 04 20.

## Medicines

- A painkiller that also brings fever down, such as paracetamol.
- An antihistamine, as tablets and as a cream, for allergies and stings.
- Sachets of oral rehydration salts, for diarrhoea and vomiting.
- The medicines each person in the household takes, with some reserve.

## How to store it

- **The first-aid supplies:** in a box with no lock, tidy and labelled, where children can't reach it.
- **The medicines, apart:** out of the sight and reach of children, away from heat, light and damp, with each pack closed.
- **Check the dates now and then.** Take expired medicines, and any that look different from how they were, to the SIGRE point at a pharmacy; don't throw them in the bin or down the drain.
