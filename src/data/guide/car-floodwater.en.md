**Never drive into a flooded street, road or ford.** About 30 cm of moving water can float a car and sweep it away, and you can't see what is underneath.

## If the car stalls and the water is rising

Decide now, while the water is still shallow:

1. **Unbuckle.** If the water is still and low (not above the wheel axles), **get out now** toward high ground, children first. If you can, open the window first; after that, the water pressure won't let you open it.
2. **If water is flowing strongly around the car** and you can't leave safely, **stay inside** and call 112. Don't walk through moving water: about 15 cm can knock you down.
3. **If water starts coming in**, get out through the window and onto the roof, children first.
4. **Don't try to restart it or save it.** The car isn't worth your life.

## If the car is floating or sinking

You have about one minute. Don't wait for it to fill with water.

1. **Seatbelt:** off.
2. **Window:** open it or break it. Don't open the door: the water would rush in.
3. **Out:** through the window, children first.
4. Once out, hold on to the car or climb onto the roof, call 112 and look for a safe way to high ground.

To break the glass, use a window breaker on a side window, best a rear one. It won't break the windscreen (laminated glass), and some front side windows can't be broken either. Keep the window breaker and a seatbelt cutter within reach.
