## When it is over

1. **Don't go out or open the windows** until the authorities say the emergency is over. The air outside may still be contaminated even if it no longer smells.
2. **The end is announced the same way as the alert:** radio and television, police or civil protection loudspeakers, official alerts on your phone and, where there are sirens, the all-clear signal (in the Basque Country, one continuous 30-second tone).
3. **If you haven't heard anything clear, stay in:** keep listening to the radio.
4. **If you were evacuated,** don't go back to the area until it is allowed.

## Opening up the house

1. Open doors and windows and turn on fans to air the whole house.
2. Take the plastic, tape and cloths off windows, doors and vents.
3. Don't go down to basements until you know no gas is left in them.
4. Clothes you took off because they were contaminated: leave them in their sealed bag and ask the authorities what to do with them.
5. Ask the town council how to clean outside: yard, balcony, vegetable garden.
6. If it still smells of gas or chemicals, call 112.

## Water

- **Don't drink tap water** until the town council or the water company say you can. Until then, drink bottled water or the water you had stored.
- A release into the air rarely affects mains water straight away, but it can contaminate water from wells, cisterns and open tanks, and water collected from roofs or tarpaulins.
- **Boiling water does not remove chemicals.**

## Garden, eggs and milk

- Food that was inside the house, especially packaged or covered food, is safe to eat.
- **Don't eat fruit or vegetables from the garden, or eggs or milk from animals that were outdoors,** until the authorities say whether and how they can be eaten. Chemicals stay on fruit skins and leaves, and animals that eat grass can pass them into meat, eggs and milk.

## Symptoms

- If someone finds it hard to breathe or is very unwell, call 112.
- If in the following hours you notice a cough, itchy eyes or skin, headache or dizziness, see a doctor or call your health centre and say you were in the area of the leak.
