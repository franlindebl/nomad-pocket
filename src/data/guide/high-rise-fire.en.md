## Fire inside a workplace building

- Raise the alarm using the call points.
- Evacuate following the marked routes and emergency exits.
- If there is no fire or thick smoke, go down by the stairs and never use the lifts.
- Make yourself seen at the window if you cannot get out of the building.
- In an emergency, call 112.
- Find out the instructions in case of fire at your workplace.

## Fire inside a home

- Stay calm and call 112 as soon as possible.
- If you can get out, close doors and windows and do not stop to pick up personal belongings.
- If there is thick smoke, crawl and, if possible, breathe through a damp cloth.
- If there is no fire or thick smoke, go down by the stairs and never use the lifts.
- If you cannot get out, protect yourself from the fire by closing all the doors and putting wet clothes in the gaps.
- Show yourself on the balcony or at the window to make yourself visible.
- Help the most vulnerable people during the evacuation.
- Have an escape plan for your home ready in case of fire and agree on a meeting point outside.
