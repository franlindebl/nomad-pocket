## Insurance: the Consorcio de Compensación de Seguros

1. **Check whether you have a policy** covering damage to property (home, community, shop, car…) or life or accident cover, with any insurer. The Consorcio pays for damage from extraordinary risks (extraordinary flood, earthquake, tsunami, volcanic eruption, tornado, winds with gusts above 120 km/h) only if the policy was in force and the premium paid up to date.
2. **Report it as soon as you can.** Call 900 222 665 (9:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday; also in English and French) or use the Consorcio's website. You can also ask your insurer, agent or broker to do it. Have the insurer and the policy number ready; for a car, make, model and number plate, and the workshop if it is in one.
3. **If the damage was caused by wind or a tornado**, send the claim to your insurer: it handles it.
4. **Before anything is taken away**, keep the remains of the damaged items for the assessor. If you can't, take photos before they are removed.
5. **Keep the invoices** for any urgent repair.
6. **When the assessor comes**, have ready the quotes and invoices, two copies (original and copy) of the policy and of the premium receipt, and a bank document showing the IBAN you want to be paid into.

## Public aid

1. If the disaster is serious, the State may give aid to families with personal injury or damage to their home and belongings, to shops and businesses with fewer than fifty employees, and to owners' communities.
2. This aid tops up what insurance covers; it does not replace it.
3. It is requested at the Delegación or Subdelegación del Gobierno of the province where the disaster happened, with the official form and the documents it asks for. The general deadline is one month from the day after the event ended.
4. Also ask your town hall and your regional government (comunidad autónoma) about other aid and about the deadline that applies to your case.
