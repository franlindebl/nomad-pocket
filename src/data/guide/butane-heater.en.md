1. **Turn the heater off before you sleep** and whenever you go out. Never sleep with a butane heater on, even if the room seems ventilated: carbon monoxide can build up, and you can't smell or see it.
2. **Use it only in a ventilated room**, with a window slightly open. Don't use it in bathrooms or in small closed rooms.
3. **Keep it 1 metre from curtains, furniture and clothes.** Don't dry clothes on it.
4. **Check the bottle and the hose.** Look at the hose's date and make sure it has no cracks. Keep the bottle upright. Change it outdoors or in a ventilated place, with no flames nearby.
5. **Don't heat the home with a gas oven or stove.** It is dangerous because of carbon monoxide and the risk of fire.
6. **Install a battery-powered CO detector** near where you sleep.

## If someone feels unwell

Headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion or drowsiness: turn the heater off if you can do it at once, get everyone out into the open air and call 112. Don't go back in until you are told it is safe.

## If the flame goes out or you smell gas

Close the bottle's valve, open the windows and don't light flames or touch switches. Do not relight it or use switches until the smell has completely gone. If the smell persists or someone has a headache or dizziness, leave and call 112. If the smell is strong, leave and follow the steps on the gas leak card.
